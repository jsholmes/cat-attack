# Dino Attack: 20 ideas to make it more fun (playtest 2026-11-23)

What I saw playing on an iPad-sized screen: a careful player goes from baby to fully grown in about 2 minutes (my T-Rex and Duckbill runs never died once they dodged hunters). Size 10 is the flattest part: everything around you is tiny, so there is nothing left to fear or chase except the egg. Plant eaters have the least to do, because beating a dino gives them nothing. One bite from something bigger sends you all the way back to a baby, with no warning beyond a roar.

## Danger and excitement
1. **Boss dinos.** Every so often a giant "king" dino (bigger than size 10, glowing red eyes, ground shakes) roams the map. Fully grown players can team up with their babies to take it down.
2. **Warning signs.** Footprints, shaking ground, and birds flying up before a big hunter appears, so kids can feel the fear and run instead of dying by surprise.
3. **Hiding spots.** Tall ferns or caves where small dinos can hide from big hunters. Hunters sniff around and give up.
4. **Packs.** Raptors and Gallimimus hunt in groups of 3 to 5, which is scary and realistic.
5. **Night time.** The world slowly goes dark; eyes glow in the bushes and some hunters only come out at night.

## Growing and progress
6. **A second chance.** When you get eaten, keep your stars if one of your babies is alive: you become that baby instead of starting over. Takes the sting out without removing the danger.
7. **Slower last sizes.** Sizes 8 to 10 need 4 or 5 meals instead of 3, so becoming huge feels earned.
8. **Dino book.** A collection page that fills in each species you have beaten, eaten or played as, with a Kit-style fact. Gives a reason to try all 15.
9. **Unlock colors and patterns.** Grow to size 10 as a species to unlock a new skin for it (stripes, albino, dark green).
10. **Best size trophy.** Remember the biggest size and most babies you have ever had, shown on the pick screen.

## Plant eater life
11. **Tail and horn moves.** Ankylosaurus tail club knocks hunters back, Triceratops charges, Stegosaurus spikes hurt whoever bites it. Plant eaters fight differently from meat eaters.
12. **Herds.** Plant eaters walk in herds; join one and the herd helps defend you.
13. **Fruit trees and special plants.** Rare glowing fruit worth two meals, so plant eaters have something to hunt for.

## The world
14. **Landmarks.** A giant skeleton, a waterfall, a huge tree, a tar pit: places to find and show friends.
15. **Nests to defend.** Your egg is sometimes guarded by a thief (a small raptor) that tries to steal it; chase it down.
16. **Weather.** Rain storms, lightning, a dust storm in the desert that hides you.
17. **Mini-map with the egg and your babies,** since the world is huge and it is easy to lose the way.

## Babies and friends
18. **Babies grow up.** After enough meals a baby becomes a teenager that helps you fight, and later a grown-up that wanders off to start its own family.
19. **Name your baby** when it hatches, shown above its head.
20. **Big roar button.** A roar that scares away anything smaller (with a cooldown). Very satisfying on an iPad, and useful for the future multiplayer: friends can roar to each other.

## Small polish I noticed
- Now that rocks are solid, tapping a dino on the far side of a rock may make you walk straight into the rock (I did not get to test this; walking around it would feel smoother).
- The "too small to count" messages appear a lot at bigger sizes; they could be quieter.
- The death screen could show the dino that got you, roaring.
