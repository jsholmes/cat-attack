**Handwriting, verbatim:** "MY HERO IS CALLED: **cat atack**". It is spelled *atack* with one "t" in the middle, not "attack".

**Drawing inventory:** One large creature fills most of the box.
- **Head (left end):** a cat face. It has two pointed ears with inner lines, two round eyes with dots, whisker strokes on both sides, and an upturned mouth/chin. The head is wide and teardrop-shaped, tapering back into the neck.
- **Body:** a very long tube. It runs right from the head, loops up and back on itself, crosses over, curls into a hook near the top right, and then sweeps down into a **pointed, hatched tail tip** at bottom right. The whole body is covered in close cross-stripes, like bands or ribs.
- **Limbs:** two long, straight vertical sticks pass through the body, one at the left-middle and one at the right-middle. Each stick ends in a **rounded paddle at both ends, top and bottom**, and every paddle has three short lines (toes or claws?). That makes four paddle-feet in total: two pointing up and two pointing down.
- **Hook (top right):** where the body folds back, it has a curled, hooked end with a notch. It could be part of the coil, or a second feature.

**Source:** `source/page_images/kit_page_01.png`, central drawing box. Name on the line at the bottom. Crop: `source/art_crops/hero_cat_attack.png`.

**Confidence:** High for the name (as spelled), the cat head, the long striped body and the four paddle-ends. Low for what the sticks *are*, and for the hook.

**Interpretation (NOT canon):** A cat-snake. The sticks could be legs that reach both up and down (so it can walk on either side?), stilts, oars/fins, or something else entirely. "cat atack" is probably "Cat Attack". Ask before standardising the spelling, though. Kit might like "Cat Atack".

**Needs Kit:** What the up-and-down paddle sticks are, and whether the name is spelled "Cat Attack" or "Cat Atack".

**Possible implication (proposal):** The sticks are the most unusual thing about the hero. Keep them in all future art until Kit says otherwise.
