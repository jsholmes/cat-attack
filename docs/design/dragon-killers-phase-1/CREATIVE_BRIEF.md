# Creative brief — preliminary, source-grounded
**Project:** DRAGON KILLERS  
**Creative director:** Kit  
**Technical producer:** Parent  
**Stage:** Phase 1 / discover and confirm — **not approved design canon**  
**Source:** `source/Kit_completed_questionnaire_FILLED_SIDES_6pages.pdf` (Kit pp. 1–6; original duplex PDF pages 1,3,5,7,9,11)

## The game Kit appears to be inventing
Kit seems drawn to a **cat-headed, long-bodied, snake-like hero named Cat Attack**, roaming a board-like world, growing dramatically with a gigantic power-up and defeating bosses. His final game name appears to be **Dragon Killers**, and the one thing he says must be built is **a castle**. The drawings show curved movement, small collectible shapes, a round toothy enemy, a tiny-to-mega transformation and a huge two-creature showdown. This is a compelling starting point — not a finished ruleset.

> **Editorial rule:** A source observation is not the same as a design decision. When handwriting or visual meaning is unclear, preserve the ambiguity and ask Kit, rather than substituting an adult's more 'sensible' answer.

## Confirmed pillars to respect
**Hero = Cat Attack; game = Dragon Killers; gigantic transformation; boss victory; a castle.** The original drawings are concept art, not something to 'fix.' They're our primary visual source for subsequent AI art exploration.

## Key unknowns — don't fill in without asking Kit
1. Who are the dragons in *Dragon Killers*? Is Cat Attack a dragon killer, or is that just a cool title?
2. What do the round collectibles represent and do?
3. How does the castle work — starting place, arena, boss home, level, or something else?
4. What causes the special 'gigantic' form, and what can it do?
5. What does the enemy / 'you lost' / 'level up' comic mean?

## Suggested first playable experience (proposal, not canon)
A *single short arena* in which Cat Attack moves, collects Kit's custom items, can become gigantic at least once, and faces one enemy, ending with a visible celebration. The castle can start as a background or entrance **only if Kit agrees**. 'Beat all bosses' is the intended full-game goal; one small boss in the first slice is a prototyping tactic, not a change to Kit's ending.

## Evaluation of success
A good Phase 1 result lets Kit say, *“Yes! That’s my game,”* point to things that are wrong, and choose what to change. A great prototype later should make his creature look and behave recognizably like the one he drew — while remaining easy for him to revise.
