# QUESTIONS_FOR_KIT: five at most, most important first

**How to use:** Ask in order and stop whenever he's done. Question 1 alone is a good session. Have his original pages out and let him point, act it out or draw. Write down his **exact words**. Every question has a "something else?" escape. If he says "I don't know yet", that's an answer too.

---

### 1. "Who's playing with you? How do you and your friends play Dragon Killers together?"
Follow-ups only if he needs them: "At the same time, or taking turns? Is your friend a Cat Attack too, or something else?" Or: "Something totally different?"

> **For the adult:** The project goal is a game Kit plays *with his friends*, but the worksheet never asked about it. This is the single biggest decision for the first prototype. Same screen or separate devices, co-op or versus, and whether friends play other snakes or bosses all change it. The p3 box might mention "snakes" and has an unreadable first line. Ask him to read that box to you too. (Kit p3)

### 2. "Tell me the story of this comic and this picture. What happened?" 👉 point at p5 top, then p6
Then: "Who's eating who? Who leveled up? What's in this little box?" Or: "Did something else happen?"

> **For the adult:** p5 says an enemy's open mouth meets you → "you lost / Levlel up". p6 says "you omnom nom boss… you won (Rrunch!". That *looks* like "whoever bites first wins", which would be the game's main rule, but it's our guess. "You lost" next to "level up" is a real puzzle. Don't explain it for him. (Kit p5, p6)

### 3. "What are these little things? What happens when Cat Attack grabs one?" 👉 p2 bottom
Then: "Is the glowing egg one of them?" Or: "Something else?"

> **For the adult:** Collecting is half of the first prototype, and we have nine unlabeled shapes. It also tells us how he gets gigantic (p4) and maybe how he grows. (Kit p2, p4)

### 4. "What happens at the castle?" 👉 p6 bottom
Or: "Is it your home, where the bosses live, something you build, or something else?"

> **For the adult:** He says we *absolutely must* build it, but it isn't drawn anywhere. It could be a place, a goal, a level or a thing he builds. Don't offer the options unless he's stuck. (Kit p6)

### 5. "Your game is called Dragon Killers. Where are the dragons?" 👉 p6 title
Or: "Is it just a cool name?" "Something else?"

> **For the adult:** No dragon is drawn. They might be the bosses, Cat Attack might be one, or the name might just sound awesome. All of these are fine. It decides who the bosses are. (Kit p6)

---

**End with:** "Which ONE thing should we make move first: Cat Attack, a bad guy, the giant power or the castle?"

**Saved for another day** (not urgent for the first prototype): what the up-and-down paddle feet are (p1), what giant Cat Attack can do (p4), the enemy's name and trick (p3), how many bosses (p5), and whether it's spelled "Cat Attack" or "Cat Atack" (p1).
