## 4. Decisions Kit made later (with date)
Source: director's meeting on the question page, 2026-11-08. These are Kit's words as typed by John; nothing was drawn or tapped. Quoted exactly, then **my reading** (not canon).

- **2026-11-08 · How friends play:** "You play by yourself against AI snakes. Other people can compete, but you have to do a thing where you have 2 snakes at the same time. When two people play at the same time, they have to stand at a QR code and put in a password so they can both play together. Then they get to play together as a game and beat the other person with that. The other person sneaks and gets killed."
  **Reading:** Solo mode is you against computer snakes. Two-player mode is versus: each player has their own snake, and they join the same game with a QR code plus a password, so probably on separate devices. Unclear: what "2 snakes at the same time" means, and whether "sneaks" is Kit's word or a typo for "snakes".
- **2026-11-08 · The p5 comic and p6 scene:** "The box down in the after slot is Level Up on the bottom. It is for if the other player wants to level up, they can. … In the first one before, there's an enemy snake and you. The enemy's mouth is open, and when you come closer and you're about to get bitten … the enemy eats you, and then you lost a level. This is a boss, and this is you, and you kill the boss. At the bottom, you won."
  **Reading:** The enemy is a snake. Getting eaten costs you a level; it is not game over. The little box is a Level Up button the other player can use. Killing the boss wins. *Replaces* the open question about "you lost / Levlel up".
- **2026-11-08 · Collectibles (p2):** "Those are little dragons with snake eggs that you can eat, and they're bigger"
  **Reading:** The round things are little dragons and snake eggs, and you eat them. "they're bigger" might mean the big shapes are the dragons, or that you get bigger. Still to ask.
- **2026-11-08 · Castle:** "You get blocks, and you build them from winning a game, so you can build the castle."
  **Reading:** The castle is something you build up between games, using blocks you win. It is not a level or an arena.
- **2026-11-08 · Dragons:** "The dragons and snakes"
  **Reading:** This answer is incomplete. Together with the collectibles answer, the dragons may be little things you eat. Still open.
