# Cat Attack

A game invented by Kit. Play it at https://jsholmes.github.io/cat-attack/

Steer with your finger (or mouse, or arrow keys). Eat eggs and little dragons to grow, and crunch all 15 dragon bosses to win.
On an iPad, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen" to get a full-screen Cat Attack icon.

# Dino Attack

A second game, invented by Kit. Play it at https://jsholmes.github.io/cat-attack/dinos/

Pick a T-Rex, Spinosaurus, Velociraptor, Triceratops, Ankylosaurus or Duckbill and start as a baby. Drag your finger (or use the arrow keys) to walk and press the Space bar to bite and claw. On an iPad with no keyboard, tap with a second finger to claw. Eat 3 that are your size to get a star and grow. At size 10, follow the blue arrow to the egg and pick your hatchling.
